A career built by teaching myself the next layer

About

I started in banking in Alaska in 2009: a management associate program at First National Bank Alaska, a loan officer desk in Soldotna, a branch manager role in Sitka before I was thirty. I learned how money actually moves by sitting across desks from the people borrowing and lending it.

Then Merrill Lynch, where I passed the Series 7, 66, and 63 and built a book from scratch, and a fee-only RIA where the work was fiduciary by structure. Somewhere in there I finished an MBA.

In 2018 I went independent, and three years of consulting landed me at equipifi, where I went from contractor to Lead Product Manager for Underwriting and built a consumer underwriting model from zero. It was the first time I had to learn the technical stack as deeply as the financial logic, and it changed how I work.

Today I'm at Cardone Ventures, where the work started as a one-person data department inside finance and has grown into the whole transaction of business: customers, payments, money, and the systems that connect them. I write the SQL, design the Power BI, ship the React, and stand up the Azure infrastructure. On the side I run Adept Advisors, teaching owner-operators how to actually use AI in their businesses.
