§ 01 — About

A career built by teaching myself the next layer.

Banking
Wealth Mgmt
Fintech Product
Data Platform
Full-Stack

I started in banking in Alaska in 2009, working my way through a management associate program at First National Bank of Alaska, into a loan officer seat in Soldotna, and then into a branch manager role in Sitka before I was thirty. I learned how money actually moves through real systems by sitting across desks from the people borrowing and lending it.

From there I went to Merrill Lynch, passed the Series 7, 66, and 63, and worked as a financial advisor in Anchorage. Then to Empirical Wealth Management, a fee-only RIA, where the work was more aligned with how I think about a client's interests. Somewhere in there I finished an MBA at the University of Maryland.

In 2018 I went independent, and three years of consulting eventually landed me at equipifi, where I went from contractor to Lead Product Manager for Underwriting. I built their consumer underwriting model from scratch — algorithms, data mappings to five core banking systems, point-of-sale credit decisioning. It was the first time I had to learn the technical stack as deeply as the financial logic, and it changed how I work.

Today I'm at Cardone Ventures, functioning as a one-person data department inside the finance function. I write the SQL, design the Power BI, ship the React, and stand up the Azure infrastructure. Finance fluency, full-stack execution, no translation layer.

On the side I run Adept Advisors, a small AI consultancy for owner-operated businesses. The model is forward-deployed: sit inside the business, find the two or three workflows where AI actually pays for itself, and build them.

"I'd rather build the thing than draw a picture of the thing."
